Mt Chimney

TIP

No wild Pokémon in the grass here. Time of day: M = morning (6–10), D = day (10–19), E = evening (19–20), N = night (20–6).

Event 1: UNCLE and the volcano

UNCLE is pouring the Meteorite's power into the crater. UNC G's grunts hold off his Flamingos while you take the cable car up. Fight UNCLE: Staryu, Flamigo and his ace Mr. Mime. Second UNCLE fight.

CAUTION

Mr. Mime is Psychic/Fairy with Filter. Steel or Poison moves are your best answer; Ghost and Dark work too.

Event 2: After the battle

Take the Meteorite and return it to Cozmo in Fallarbor for a TM. The Lava Cookie stand up here sells full-status cures. Jagged Pass leads down to Lavaridge.

Event 3: Finale (later)

After the Sootopolis crisis UNCLE comes back to this crater alone. Beat his real team (Starmie, Weezing, Flamigo, Vanilluxe, Mr. Mime) and he finally learns to share: he gives you his Vanillite.
