Pikachu (Phd)

Mouse Pokémon
Electric
Abilities: Static · hidden: Lightning Rod
Height: 0.4 m · Weight: 6.0 kg · Gender: Always female
Egg groups: No Eggs Discovered · Catch rate: 190

It stores electricity in the electric sacs on its cheeks. When it releases pent-up energy in a burst, the electric power is equal to a lightning bolt.

Other forms: Pikachu, Pikachu (Cosplay), Pikachu (Rock Star), Pikachu (Belle), Pikachu (Pop Star), Pikachu (Libre), Pikachu (Original), Pikachu (Hoenn), Pikachu (Sinnoh), Pikachu (Unova), Pikachu (Kalos), Alolan Pikachu, Pikachu (Partner), Pikachu (World), Pikachu (Starter).
