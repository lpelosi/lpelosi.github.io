TIP

Beach to the south, Petalburg Woods in the middle. DAD's first checkpoint is here once you hold a badge. Wild Pokémon here are LV3–5. Time of day: M = morning (6–10), D = day (10–19), E = evening (19–20), N = night (20–6).

Event: DAD

DAD's first checkpoint. He tests you once you hold one badge; win for Exp. Candies.

DAD: Lotad LV13; Rookidee LV14

Team shown for Normal. Hard adds items and full sets; Easy is three levels lower.
