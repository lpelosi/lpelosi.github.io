TIP

No wild Pokémon in the grass here. Time of day: M = morning (6–10), D = day (10–19), E = evening (19–20), N = night (20–6).

Event 1: Dive point

At the west end of Route 134, past the currents, a dark patch of water marks the way down to the Sealed Chamber. Bring Relicanth first in the party and Wailord last.
