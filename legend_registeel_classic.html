Registeel

Registeel

Level 40 · Steel
Where: Ancient Tomb
Unlock: Sealed Chamber opened (7 badges for Dive)

How to get it

Ancient Tomb on Route 120, south of the bridges. Stand in the centre of the room and use Flash (Field Case). The door opens; Registeel is in the back room.

Emerald's own event, unchanged.

Side quest "The Regi Trio and Regigigas": Ancient Tomb. Reached from: Route 120.
