TIP

Wild Pokémon here are LV27–33. Time of day: M = morning (6–10), D = day (10–19), E = evening (19–20), N = night (20–6).

Event 1: Flamingo Hideout, side rooms

The hideout's side chambers hold Paradox and Ultra Beasts after eight badges: Blacephalon here on 3F. Heatran sits in Groudon's old chamber on 4F from six badges, Reshiram on 1F after the Hall of Fame.
