No wild Pokémon in the grass here. Time of day: M = morning (6–10), D = day (10–19), E = evening (19–20), N = night (20–6).

Event 1: Gym Battle: WINONA

Flying-type behind rotating gates. Electric, Rock and Ice moves; Altaria is the ace and part Dragon, so Ice hits it hardest. Reward: Feather Badge.

CAUTION

Cap is 33. Skarmory walls physical attackers; a special Fire or Electric move deals with it.
