TIP

No wild Pokémon in the grass here. Time of day: M = morning (6–10), D = day (10–19), E = evening (19–20), N = night (20–6).

Event 1: The roof

Rayquaza returns here after the story and can be caught (level 70). Arceus and Lunala join it after the Hall of Fame. Dialga (2F) and Palkia (4F) sit on the in-between floors.

CAUTION

Rayquaza's Air Lock removes weather. Ice moves, and something that can survive an Outrage.
