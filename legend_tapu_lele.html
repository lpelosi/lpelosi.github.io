Tapu Lele

Tapu Lele

Level 40 · PsychicFairy
Where: Route 121
Unlock: 6 badges (after the Fortree Gym)
Balls: Ultra Balls (catch rate 60)

How to get it

No event and no puzzle. It stands in the open at the ★ on the map: walk up, face it and press A to battle.

Catch it and it is gone for good. Knock it out or run and it is back the next time you enter the map.

Walkthrough step 53: Route 121. Reached from: Route 122, Route 120, Lilycove City.
