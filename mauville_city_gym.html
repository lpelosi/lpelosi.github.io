TIP

No wild Pokémon in the grass here. Time of day: M = morning (6–10), D = day (10–19), E = evening (19–20), N = night (20–6).

Event 1: Gym Battle: WATTSON

Electric-type behind a switch puzzle. Ground types are immune to his whole team; Rock and Grass take hits well. Manectric is the ace. Reward: Dynamo Badge.

CAUTION

Cap is 24. Magneton's Steel typing shrugs off Normal and Flying moves; bring Fire or Ground.
