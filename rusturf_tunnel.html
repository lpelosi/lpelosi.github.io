TIP

Wild Pokémon here are LV5–8. Time of day: M = morning (6–10), D = day (10–19), E = evening (19–20), N = night (20–6).

Event 1: Flamingo grunt

The grunt is cornered at the end of the tunnel with Peeko. Beat him, take the goods back, and Mr. Briney offers his boat in thanks. The rockfall deeper in needs Rock Smash: you can clear it later from Verdanturf.
