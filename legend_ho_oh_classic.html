Ho-Oh

Level 70 · FireFlying
Where: Navel Rock Top
Unlock: Champion + Mystic Ticket

How to get it

Mystic Ticket from the Concierge, ferry from Lilycove Harbor. From the fork inside Navel Rock take the stairs up through four rooms to the summit.

Emerald's own event, unchanged.

Side quest "The ferry tickets: Southern Island, Zarude, Deoxys, Ho-Oh and Lugia": Navel Rock Top.
