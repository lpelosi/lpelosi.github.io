Event 1: UNC G's last flight

Back in Slateport, UNC G sits on the harbor dock waiting for a boat out of Hoenn. She wants one more battle with her real team: Gyarados, Milotic, Oricorio, Alolan Muk, Jynx. Win, tell her what she is missing, and she gives you her Lapras and invites herself to dinner.
