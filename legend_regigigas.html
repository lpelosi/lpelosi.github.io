Regigigas

Regigigas

Level 50 · Normal
Where: Sealed Chamber Inner Room
Unlock: 6 badges (after the Fortree Gym)

How to get it

Inside the Sealed Chamber's inner room, so you need the Regi quest first: Relicanth first and Wailord last in the party, Dig at the Braille wall on Route 134's seabed, then read the far tablet. Regigigas stands in the inner room from six badges (see the Regi side quest).

Catch it and it is gone for good. Knock it out or run and it is back the next time you enter the map.

Side quest "The Regi Trio and Regigigas": Sealed Chamber Inner Room.
