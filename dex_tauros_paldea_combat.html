Paldean Tauros

Wild Bull Pokémon
Fighting
Abilities: Intimidate, Anger Point · hidden: Cud Chew
Height: 1.4 m · Weight: 115.0 kg · Gender: Always male
Egg groups: Field · Catch rate: 45

This Pokémon has a muscular body and excels at close-quarters combat. It uses its short horns to strike the opponent's weak spots.
