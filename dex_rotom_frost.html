Rotom (Frost)

Plasma Pokémon
ElectricIce
Abilities: Levitate
Height: 0.3 m · Weight: 0.3 kg · Gender: Genderless
Egg groups: Amorphous · Catch rate: 45

In this form, Rotom likes to play freezing cold pranks. You may find it's turned the bath you just filled to solid ice! It battles by spewing cold air.

Other forms: Rotom, Rotom (Heat), Rotom (Wash), Rotom (Fan), Rotom (Mow).
