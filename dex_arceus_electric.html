Alpha Pokémon
Electric
Abilities: Multitype
Height: 3.2 m · Weight: 320.0 kg · Gender: Genderless
Egg groups: No Eggs Discovered · Catch rate: 3

It is told in mythology that this Pokémon emerged from an egg and shaped all there is in this world, before the universe even existed.
