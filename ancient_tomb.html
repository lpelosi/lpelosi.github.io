Ancient Tomb

TIP

No wild Pokémon in the grass here. Time of day: M = morning (6–10), D = day (10–19), E = evening (19–20), N = night (20–6).

Event 1: Registeel

Route 120, south of the bridges. Stand in the middle of the room and use Fly (yes, from the Field Case). Registeel is Steel: Fire, Fighting or Ground.

Event 2: Hoopa (Champion)

Hoopa appears here after the Hall of Fame. Level 65 Psychic/Ghost; Dark moves.
