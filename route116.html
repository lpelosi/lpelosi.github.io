Event 1: The stolen goods

Leaving Rustboro, a Devon employee runs up: a Flamingo grunt took the Devon Goods and ran into Rusturf Tunnel. Follow the route east; Peeko the Wingull's owner Mr. Briney is waiting outside the tunnel.
