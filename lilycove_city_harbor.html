Lilycove City Harbor

TIP

No wild Pokémon in the grass here. Time of day: M = morning (6–10), D = day (10–19), E = evening (19–20), N = night (20–6).

Event 1: Board the ferry

Talk to the sailor at the counter. The S.S. TIDAL runs after the Hall of Fame; with the tickets in your bag the destination list adds SOUTHERN ISLAND, FARAWAY ISLAND, BIRTH ISLAND and NAVEL ROCK. Sail back the same way from each island's dock.
