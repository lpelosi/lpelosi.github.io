Kyurem

Boundary Pokémon
DragonIce
Abilities: Pressure
Height: 3.0 m · Weight: 325.0 kg · Gender: Genderless
Egg groups: No Eggs Discovered · Catch rate: 3

This legendary ice Pokémon waits for a hero to fill in the missing parts of its body with truth or ideals. Its body became frozen when its energy leaked out.

Other forms: Kyurem (White), Kyurem (Black).

‹ PokédexNo. 646

Where to get it

Legendary encounter: Shoal Cave Low Tide Inner Room, LV70. Unlock: Champion (after the Hall of Fame).

Available as your starter Pokémon on Route 101.
