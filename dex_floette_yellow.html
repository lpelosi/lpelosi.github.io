Single Bloom Pokémon
Fairy
Abilities: Flower Veil · hidden: Symbiosis
Height: 0.2 m · Weight: 0.9 kg · Gender: Always female
Egg groups: Fairy · Catch rate: 120

This Pokémon can draw forth the power hidden within yellow flowers. This power then becomes the moves Floette uses to protect itself.

Other forms: Floette (Red), Floette (Orange), Floette (Blue), Floette (White), Floette (Eternal).
