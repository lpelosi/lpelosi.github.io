Deoxys

Deoxys

Level 30 ·
Where: Birth Island Exterior
Unlock: Champion + Aurora Ticket

How to get it

Aurora Ticket from the Concierge, ferry from Lilycove Harbor. Solve the triangle rock puzzle (eleven presses, exact route on the Birth Island page); Deoxys comes down when the rock shatters.

Emerald's own event, unchanged.

Side quest "The ferry tickets: Southern Island, Mew, Deoxys, Ho-Oh and Lugia": Birth Island Exterior.
