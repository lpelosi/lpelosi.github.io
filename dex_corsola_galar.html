Galarian Corsola

Coral Pokémon
Ghost
Abilities: Weak Armor · hidden: Cursed Body
Height: 0.6 m · Weight: 0.5 kg · Gender: 75% female
Egg groups: Water 1 / Water 3 · Catch rate: 60

Sudden climate change wiped out this ancient kind of Corsola. With its branches, it absorbs others' life-force. It curses those unaware that kick it by accident.

Other forms: Corsola.
