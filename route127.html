TIP

No wild Pokémon in the grass here. Time of day: M = morning (6–10), D = day (10–19), E = evening (19–20), N = night (20–6).

Event 1: Dive spots

Steven gave you Dive at his house; the Field Case has it too. Dark patches of water are dive points.
