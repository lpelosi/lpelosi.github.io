TIP

Wild Pokémon here are LV10–12. Time of day: M = morning (6–10), D = day (10–19), E = evening (19–20), N = night (20–6).

Event 1: Steven

Hand Steven the letter. He gives you TM Steel Wing and disappears again; you will keep bumping into him all game. Nothing else down here but wild Pokémon, including Sableye and Mawile at night.
