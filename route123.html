TIP

Wild Pokémon here are LV25–28. Time of day: M = morning (6–10), D = day (10–19), E = evening (19–20), N = night (20–6).

Event 1: Berry Master

The Berry Master's house gives two berries a day; his wife gives a rare one for the right phrase. Tapu Bulu (six badges) and Calyrex (Champion) stand in the grass.
