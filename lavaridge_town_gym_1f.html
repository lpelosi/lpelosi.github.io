TIP

No wild Pokémon in the grass here. Time of day: M = morning (6–10), D = day (10–19), E = evening (19–20), N = night (20–6).

Event 1: Gym Battle: FLANNERY

Fire-type. Water, Ground and Rock types walk through it; watch Torkoal's Overheat and White Smoke. Reward: Heat Badge, and your rival meets you outside with the Go-Goggles.

CAUTION

Cap is 29. Sunny Day boosts her whole team; a Rain Dance user or a bulky Water type ends it fast.
