Zygarde (50)

Zygarde (50)

Level 70 · DragonGround
Where: Desert Ruins
Unlock: Champion (after the Hall of Fame)

How to get it

In the Desert Ruins' Regirock room (Champion). Do the Regirock puzzle first if the door is still shut: step right twice, down twice, then Strength on the wall.

Catch it and it is gone for good. Knock it out or run and it is back the next time you enter the map.

Side quest "The Regi Trio and Regigigas": Desert Ruins. Reached from: Route 111.
