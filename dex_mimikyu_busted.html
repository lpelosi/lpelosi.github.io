After going to all the effort of disguising itself, its neck was broken. Whatever is inside is probably unharmed, but it's still feeling sad.

Other forms: Mimikyu (Disguised).

‹ PokédexNo. 778

Where to get it

A form of Mimikyu (Disguised): get the base Pokémon first, the form change follows.

How to get this form

  • In battle, after hurting itself in confusion
  • In battle, after being hit by a physical move
  • In battle, after being hit by a special move

Evolution
