Lunala

Lunala

Level 70 · PsychicGhost
Where: Sky Pillar Top
Unlock: Champion (after the Hall of Fame)

How to get it

Roof of the Sky Pillar (Champion), on the other side of the roof from Arceus.

Catch it and it is gone for good. Knock it out or run and it is back the next time you enter the map.

Side quest "Sky Pillar, floor by floor": Sky Pillar Top.
