Lilycove City

‹ Safari Zone SouthStep 55 of 95Next: Route 122 ›

TIP

No wild Pokémon in the grass here. Time of day: M = morning (6–10), D = day (10–19), E = evening (19–20), N = night (20–6).

Event 1: Rival, one more time

Your rival ambushes you outside the Department Store with a full team. After this they retire to Littleroot.

Event 2: Horizon Hideout

The cove on the east side is Équipe Horizon's base, but a grunt blocks it until you have been to Mt. Pyre. Route 122 is south.
