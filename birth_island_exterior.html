TIP

No wild Pokémon in the grass here. Time of day: M = morning (6–10), D = day (10–19), E = evening (19–20), N = night (20–6).

Event 1: Deoxys

Level 30. The triangle rock puzzle from Emerald, unchanged. Face the rock and press A from each of the spots in the classic order (below it first, then the left and right edges, the upper corners and the centre); the triangle changes when a step is right and resets when one is wrong. Deoxys appears in front of the rock when it is solved.
