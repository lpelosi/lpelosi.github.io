TIP

No wild Pokémon in the grass here. Time of day: M = morning (6–10), D = day (10–19), E = evening (19–20), N = night (20–6).

Event 1: Zarude

Level 65. The jungle island's hide-and-seek Pokémon is Zarude: it hides in the tall grass and mirrors your movement, keeping its distance, so keep following until it stops in the open, then talk to it. Dark/Grass: Fairy, Fire, Ice, Flying or Bug. Save first.
