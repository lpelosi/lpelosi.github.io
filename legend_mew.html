Mew

Mew

Level 10 · Psychic
Where: Petalburg Woods
Unlock: 1 badge (after the Rustboro Gym)
Balls: Poké Balls work (catch rate 200, like a Zigzagoon)

How to get it

Your first mythical: level 10 in Petalburg Woods as soon as you have the Stone Badge. No puzzle, and a plain Poké Ball will do. It learns Transform early and every TM later, so it grows with you.

Catch it and it is gone for good. Knock it out or run and it is back the next time you enter the map.

Walkthrough step 8: Petalburg Woods. Reached from: Route 104.
