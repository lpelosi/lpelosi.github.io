Groudon

Groudon

Level 70 · Ground
Where: Terra Cave End
Unlock: Champion

How to get it

Talk to the scientist on the Weather Institute's 1F: he reports intense sunlight on one land route (114, 115, 116 or 118). A cave, the Terra Cave, has opened there. Groudon sleeps at the end of it.

Emerald's own event, unchanged.

Side quest "Kyogre and Groudon return": Terra Cave End.
