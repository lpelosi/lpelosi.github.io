Petalburg City

‹ Route 102Step 6 of 95Next: Route 104 ›

TIP

No wild Pokémon in the grass here. Time of day: M = morning (6–10), D = day (10–19), E = evening (19–20), N = night (20–6).

Event 1: DAD and Wally

Step into the gym and DAD asks you to help Wally catch his first Pokémon on Route 102 (a scripted Ralts catch). DAD is your father: supportive, keen to see you grow. He will not fight you here until you hold four badges, but he sets up checkpoints on Route 104, 110 and 120 to test you along the way.

Event 2: Wally's father

The house left of the gym belongs to Wally's parents. Come back after your fifth badge for the Surf HM (you won't need it: the Field Case has Surf), or just to hear how Wally is doing.
