Wild Pokémon here are LV27–33. Time of day: M = morning (6–10), D = day (10–19), E = evening (19–20), N = night (20–6).

Event 1: UNCLE and Groudon

UNCLE uses the Blue Orb to wake Groudon; it flees. He fights you anyway: Starmie, Koffing, Flamigo, Mr. Mime. Third UNCLE fight. Afterwards he heads for Mossdeep.
