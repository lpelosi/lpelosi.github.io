TIP

Wild Pokémon here are LV24–30. Time of day: M = morning (6–10), D = day (10–19), E = evening (19–20), N = night (20–6).

Event 1: UNC G and the orbs

UNCLE has already taken the Blue Orb. UNC G lifts the Red Orb ('it matches my shoes') and asks for one dance before she flies: Pelipper, Oricorio, an Alolan Grimer and her ace Jynx. Second UNC G fight.

CAUTION

Jynx is Ice/Psychic with Lovely Kiss. Steel, Rock or Fire moves; do not lead with something slow.
