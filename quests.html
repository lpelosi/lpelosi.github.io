Side Quests

TIP

Everything outside the badge path, earliest first: the Regi puzzle, fossils, the tides at Shoal Cave, and the towers and caves that fill with legendaries at 6 badges, 8 badges and after the Hall of Fame. Each quest shows the badges you need and a suggested level, then walks you through its maps in order. Quests marked POST-GAME need the Hall of Fame. Every area page also lists the legendaries standing on it (★ on the map).

Shoal Cave and the tides

Requires: 6 badgesSuggested LV35–50Reached by Surf from Route 124/125 once Lilycove is open

The cave north of Mossdeep changes with the tide (high tide 9-3, low tide 3-9, real clock). Four Shoal Salt and four Shoal Shell make a Shell Bell at the entrance; the ice room at the bottom holds the legendaries.

  • 1Shoal Cave Low Tide Stairs Room
  • 2Shoal Cave Low Tide Lower Room
  • 3Shoal Cave Low Tide Inner Room
  • 4Shoal Cave Low Tide Ice Room

The Abandoned Ship and the Scanner

Requires: 7 badgesSuggested LV30–40Dive (7th badge) to reach the hidden floor

The wreck on Route 108 has a second half you can only reach by diving. It ends with Capt. Stern's Scanner and one of the two deep-sea items.

  • 1Abandoned Ship Hidden Floor Corridors
  • 2Slateport Shipyard: Capt. Stern

Pacifidlog, Mirage Island and the trade boat

Requires: 7 badgesSuggested LV35–45Surf there from Route 129; Manaphy after the Hall of Fame

The floating town between Routes 131 and 132 is small, but it is the only way to some things.

  • 1Pacifidlog Town

The Regi Trio and Regigigas

Requires: 7 badgesSuggested LV40–50Dive (7th badge) for the Route 134 chamber; Regigigas from 6 badges; Zygarde and Hoopa after the Hall of Fame

The Braille puzzle from the original Emerald, still here. You need a Relicanth (Route 124 or 126 underwater) and a Wailord (Wailmer evolves at 40), then three trips across Hoenn. Regigigas waits in the inner chamber once you hold six badges, with Zygarde and Hoopa joining the two outer ruins after you become Champion.

  • 1Route 134
  • 2Sealed Chamber Outer Room
  • 3Sealed Chamber Inner Room
  • 4Desert Ruins
  • 5Island Cave
  • 6Ancient Tomb

Meteor Falls: the deep rooms

Requires: 8 badgesSuggested LV45–55Waterfall (8th badge); Steven's cave and Giratina after the Hall of Fame

Past the story cave, Meteor Falls goes down two more levels. Bagon lives in the back room, and after you are Champion Steven waits in his own cave for the hardest rematch in the game outside the family.

  • 2Meteor Falls B1F 1R

The ferry tickets: Southern Island, Mew, Deoxys, Ho-Oh and Lugia
