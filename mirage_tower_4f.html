TIP

Wild Pokémon here are LV20–24. Time of day: M = morning (6–10), D = day (10–19), E = evening (19–20), N = night (20–6).

Event 1: The fossils

Pick one. Rock Smash the tube and grab it; the tower falls apart around you and you land on Route 111 with the fossil. Roaring Moon lives up here later.
