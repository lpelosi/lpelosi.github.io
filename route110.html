TIP

Wild Pokémon here are LV12–13. Time of day: M = morning (6–10), D = day (10–19), E = evening (19–20), N = night (20–6).

Event 1: Trick House

Optional puzzle house with a TM at the end of each round. Comes back with new puzzles after each badge.

Event 2: Rival battle

Halfway up the route your rival challenges you again: three Pokémon this time, their starter evolved.

Event 3: DAD checkpoint 2

With three badges DAD waits near the north end with Corvisquire, Lombre, Gastly and Mankey. Beat him for Exp. Candies M.
