Regirock

Regirock

Level 40 · Rock
Where: Desert Ruins
Unlock: Sealed Chamber opened (7 badges for Dive)

How to get it

Desert Ruins on Route 111. Inside: two steps right, two steps down, then Strength facing the wall. The door opens; Regirock is in the back room.

Emerald's own event, unchanged.

Side quest "The Regi Trio and Regigigas": Desert Ruins. Reached from: Route 111.
