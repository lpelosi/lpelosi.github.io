TIP

No wild Pokémon in the grass here. Time of day: M = morning (6–10), D = day (10–19), E = evening (19–20), N = night (20–6).

Event 1: Mewtwo

The lowest chamber. Mewtwo at level 70 after the Hall of Fame. Terapagos stands at the cave entrance at the same point.

CAUTION

Mewtwo's Psystrike goes through special walls. Dark types with Sucker Punch and Ultra Balls at 1 HP.
