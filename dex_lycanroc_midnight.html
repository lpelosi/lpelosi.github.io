Lycanroc (Midnight)

Wolf Pokémon
Rock
Abilities: Keen Eye, Vital Spirit · hidden: No Guard
Height: 1.1 m · Weight: 25.0 kg · Gender: 50% female
Egg groups: Field · Catch rate: 90

This Pokémon uses its rocky mane to slash any who approach. It will even disobey its Trainer if it dislikes the orders it was given.

Other forms: Lycanroc (Midday), Lycanroc (Dusk).
