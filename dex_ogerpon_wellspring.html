Ogerpon (Wellspring)

Mask Pokémon
GrassWater
Abilities: Water Absorb
Height: 1.2 m · Weight: 39.8 kg · Gender: Always female

Other forms: Ogerpon (Teal), Ogerpon (Hearthflame), Ogerpon (Cornerstone).
