Shellos (East)

Sea Slug Pokémon
Water
Abilities: Sticky Hold, Storm Drain · hidden: Sand Force
Height: 0.3 m · Weight: 6.3 kg · Gender: 50% female
Egg groups: Water 1 / Amorphous · Catch rate: 190

It's capable of spending a limited amount of time on land until their skin dries out. One theory suggests that living in cold seas causes Shellos to take on this form.

Other forms: Shellos (West).
