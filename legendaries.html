Legendary Pokémon

Every legendary, mythical, Ultra Beast and Paradox Pokémon is a static encounter: walk up, press A, battle. Catch it and it is gone for good. Knock it out or run and it flies off, but it is back the next time you enter the map. A legendary only appears once you have met its unlock requirement.

Vanilla Emerald events are unchanged: Regirock, Regice, Registeel (Sealed Chamber puzzle), Kyogre, Groudon, Rayquaza, Latias/Latios (Southern Island), Mew (Faraway Island), Deoxys (Birth Island), Lugia and Ho-Oh (Navel Rock).
