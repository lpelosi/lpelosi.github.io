Weezing

Poison Gas Pokémon
Poison
Abilities: Levitate, Neutralizing Gas · hidden: Stench
Height: 1.2 m · Weight: 9.5 kg · Gender: 50% female
Egg groups: Amorphous · Catch rate: 60
