Blacephalon

Blacephalon

Level 55 · FireGhost
Where: Magma Hideout 3F 3R
Unlock: 8 badges (after the Sootopolis Gym)
Balls: Ultra or Dusk Balls (catch rate 30)

How to get it

No event and no puzzle. It stands in the open at the ★ on the map: walk up, face it and press A to battle.

Catch it and it is gone for good. Knock it out or run and it is back the next time you enter the map.

Side quest "Everything else worth a detour": Magma Hideout 3F 3R.
