Cherrim (Sunshine)

Blossom Pokémon
Grass
Abilities: Flower Gift
Height: 0.5 m · Weight: 9.3 kg · Gender: 50% female
Egg groups: Fairy / Grass · Catch rate: 75

Cherrim takes this form on sunny days. Its faint scent entices bug Pokémon to it. It immediately closes its petals and becomes immobile once the sun hides.

Other forms: Cherrim (Overcast).
