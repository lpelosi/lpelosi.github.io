Gym Battles

CAUTION

Level caps are tied to badges. Your Pokémon stop gaining EXP at the cap until you earn the next badge (Easy and Normal can switch the cap off in EXTRAS). Teams below are the Normal versions; Hard and Easy variants are listed under each.

Gym Battle: ROXANNE

CAUTION

Your Pokémon should be around LV15 before you take on ROXANNE. Rustboro City Gym: Rock-type. Reward: Stone Badge.

Leader

Leader ROXANNE: Rolycoly LV13; Larvitar LV14; Nosepass LV15
Hard: Geodude LV13; Rolycoly LV13; Larvitar LV14; Nosepass LV15 (ITEM_ORAN_BERRY)
Easy: Geodude LV12; Geodude LV12; Nosepass LV15

Gym Battle: BRAWLY

CAUTION
