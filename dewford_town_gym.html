TIP

No wild Pokémon in the grass here. Time of day: M = morning (6–10), D = day (10–19), E = evening (19–20), N = night (20–6).

Event 1: Gym Battle: BRAWLY

Fighting-type in a dark gym that lights up as you beat his trainers. Flying, Psychic and Fairy moves rule; keep Normal and Rock types on the bench. Reward: Knuckle Badge.

CAUTION

Cap is 19. Bulk Up and Makuhita's Fake Out punish slow leads; open with something fast.
