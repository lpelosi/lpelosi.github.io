TIP

Wild Pokémon here are LV2–3. Time of day: M = morning (6–10), D = day (10–19), E = evening (19–20), N = night (20–6).

Event 1: Help me!

Prof. Birch is being chased around the grass by a wild Zigzagoon. Grab his bag and pick your starter: any evolutionary base form in the game, from Bulbasaur to Sprigatito. RANDOM picks one for you; RANDOM+ picks one that can follow you. You are also asked whether you want it shiny.

CAUTION

Pick a starter you like the look of. Every choice learns an attacking move by level 5, and the level cap keeps early gyms fair for any type.

Event 2: Rewards

Back in the lab Birch hands over the Pokédex, five Poké Balls, the DexNav, and two key items: the Field Case (use Cut, Surf, Fly and the rest without teaching them) and the PokéVial (three full heals, refilled at any Pokémon Center). The Exp. Share is on for the whole party from the start.
