TIP

No wild Pokémon in the grass here. Time of day: M = morning (6–10), D = day (10–19), E = evening (19–20), N = night (20–6).

Event 1: The Braille wall

Read the last tablet, then use Dig (the Field Case has it) facing the wall. The door to the inner room opens.
