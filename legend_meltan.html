Meltan

Meltan

Level 25 · Steel
Where: Slateport City
Unlock: 3 badges (after the Mauville Gym)

How to get it

No event and no puzzle: Meltan simply stands on the street in Slateport once you hold three badges. Walk up and press A.

Catch it and it is gone for good. Knock it out or run and it is back the next time you enter the map.

Walkthrough step 21: Slateport City. Reached from: Route 110, Route 109, Route 134.
