Exeggutor

Coconut Pokémon
GrassPsychic
Abilities: Chlorophyll · hidden: Harvest
Height: 2.0 m · Weight: 120.0 kg · Gender: 50% female
Egg groups: Grass · Catch rate: 45

Originally from the tropics, Exeggutor's heads grow larger from exposure to strong sunlight. It is said that when the heads fall, they group to form an Exeggcute.

Other forms: Alolan Exeggutor.
