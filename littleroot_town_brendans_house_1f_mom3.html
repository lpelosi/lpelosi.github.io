Event 1: Go home: MOM's third battle

Eight badges: MOM's full setlist. Altaria, Primarina, Toxtricity, Jigglypuff and HUBERT. Three Exp. Candies XL for the win. Do this before the family dinner; she will not host it until this one is done.

CAUTION

Toxtricity's Boomburst hits everything hard. A Ground type takes it and Altaria without much fuss.
