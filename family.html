The Family

TIP

Emma's Emerald swaps Hoenn's villains, gym leader, Elite Four and Champion for your own family. Team Magma is the Frozen Flamingos, Team Aqua is Équipe Horizon, and both bosses are your dad's siblings; the Elite Four are your grandparents and the Champion is your older sibling. After the story everyone moves into your Littleroot house: DAD, the sibling, UNCLE and UNC G, and a visiting grandparent most days, each with a daily battle. On your birthday (girl: 7 September, boy: 18 August) the whole family is home and MOM has a present.

UNCLE

Boss of the Frozen Flamingos (replaces Maxie)

Your dad's brother. Cool, radical, and constitutionally unable to share: rule one is finders keepers. He wants the Meteorite, the volcano and Groudon for himself, and only figures out why he keeps losing when you beat him for the fifth time.

Encounters

1Petalburg Woods: Mime Jr., Flamigo
2Mt. Chimney: Staryu, Flamigo, Mr. Mime
3Flamingo Hideout: Starmie, Koffing, Flamigo, Mr. Mime
4Space Center (tag battle with Steven): Starmie, Weezing, Flamigo
5Mt. Chimney, after Sootopolis: Starmie, Weezing, Flamigo, Vanilluxe, Mr. Mime

Ace: Mr. Mime. Gift after the final fight: his Vanillite, the first Pokémon he ever caught.

UNC G

Boss of Équipe Horizon (replaces Archie)

Your aunt, DAD's baby sister. A globetrotting jetsetter with a martini in one hand and a passport full of stamps, she wants Kyogre to flood Hoenn so the sea goes on forever. She has been everywhere and never once gone home.

Encounters

1Oceanic Museum: Oricorio (Pom-Pom), Smoochum
2Mt. Pyre summit: Pelipper, Oricorio (Sensu), Alolan Grimer, Jynx
3Seafloor Cavern: Gyarados, Oricorio (Pa'u), Alolan Muk, Jynx
4Slateport Harbor dock, after Sootopolis: Gyarados, Milotic, Oricorio (Baile), Alolan Muk, Jynx

Ace: Jynx, then Alolan Muk. A different outfit in every fight. Gift after the final fight: her Lapras. Lesson: travel far, but come home to family.

DAD

Petalburg Gym Leader (replaces Norman)

Supportive, wants you to grow and have fun. He waits on the road to test you three times before you ever reach his gym, and his Corviknight is with him from the gym battle on.

Encounters

1Route 104 (1 badge): Rookidee line, Lotad
2Route 110 (3 badges): Corvisquire, Lombre, Gastly, Mankey
3Petalburg Gym (5th badge): Corviknight, Ludicolo, Haunter, Primeape, Phanpy
4Route 120 (6 badges): Corviknight, Ludicolo, Gengar, Primeape, Donphan

Rewards: Exp. Candies at each checkpoint, the Balance Badge at the gym.

COLIN and EMMA

The Champion, and your big brother or sister

Colin (if you play as the girl) or Emma (if you play as the boy) left home before your journey began and has been Hoenn's Champion ever since. They watched the whole family fight you and want no favours. Win and they come home for good, with a daily rematch waiting in the living room.

Encounters

1Champion's room (girl player): Colin: Typhlosion, Lucario and a sun team
2Champion's room (boy player): Emma: Vanilluxe, Raichu and a snow team
3Littleroot house, post-game: Daily rematch with the same team

Colin's ace is Lucario, Emma's is Vanilluxe. NANNY is deliberately stronger than either.

MOM

Battles you at home

Loves her family and her music. Stays in Littleroot, but asks for a battle every time you come home with a new milestone, and hosts the family dinner battle once UNCLE and UNC G have both learned their lessons.

Encounters

1Littleroot house (2 badges): Oricorio, Jigglypuff, HUBERT
2Littleroot house (5 badges): Swablu, Brionne, Jigglypuff, HUBERT
3Littleroot house (8 badges): Altaria, Primarina, Toxtricity, Jigglypuff, HUBERT
4Family dinner (after both sibling finals): Altaria, Primarina, Toxtricity, Jigglypuff, HUBERT

Ace: HUBERT the Lillipup, who never evolves. Gift after the dinner battle: an Igglybuff. UNCLE and UNC G then live in your living room and offer a daily double battle (holiday outfits in December).
