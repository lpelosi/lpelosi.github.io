Calyrex (Ice)

High King Pokémon
PsychicIce
Abilities: As One
Height: 2.4 m · Weight: 809.1 kg · Gender: Genderless
Egg groups: No Eggs Discovered · Catch rate: 3

According to lore, this Pokémon showed no mercy to those who got in its way, yet it would heal its opponents' wounds after battle.

Other forms: Calyrex, Calyrex (Shadow).

  • Fuse Calyrex with Glastrier using Reins of Unity
