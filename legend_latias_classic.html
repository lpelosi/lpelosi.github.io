Latias

Latias

Level 50 · DragonPsychic
Where: Southern Island Interior
Unlock: Champion + Eon Ticket

How to get it

Eon Ticket from the Concierge, ferry from Lilycove Harbor. Latias waits here if you told MOM BLUE after the Hall of Fame (Latios then roams Hoenn); RED swaps them. Holds a Soul Dew.

Emerald's own event, unchanged.

Side quest "The ferry tickets: Southern Island, Zarude, Deoxys, Ho-Oh and Lugia": Southern Island Interior.
