Scatterbug (Pokeball)

Scatterdust Pokémon
Bug
Abilities: Shield Dust, Compound Eyes · hidden: Friend Guard
Height: 0.3 m · Weight: 2.5 kg · Gender: 50% female
Egg groups: Bug · Catch rate: 255

When under attack from bird Pokémon, it spews a poisonous black powder that causes paralysis on contact. Scatterbug can live in any region or climate.
