TIP

Wild Pokémon here are LV20–24. Time of day: M = morning (6–10), D = day (10–19), E = evening (19–20), N = night (20–6).

Event 1: Mirage Tower

The tower flickers in and out of the desert sandstorm on Route 111 (north end). Cracked floors need the Mach Bike at full speed. The Root Fossil and Claw Fossil sit on the top floor; take one and the tower collapses.

Event 2: Paradox Pokémon (8 badges)

Once you hold eight badges each floor has Paradox Pokémon standing in the sand: Great Tusk and Scream Tail on 1F, Brute Bonnet and Flutter Mane on 2F, Slither Wing and Sandy Shocks on 3F, Roaring Moon on 4F. All level 55.
