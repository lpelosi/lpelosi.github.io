TIP

Wild Pokémon here are LV25–27. Time of day: M = morning (6–10), D = day (10–19), E = evening (19–20), N = night (20–6).

Event 1: Steven and the Kecleon

Steven hands you the Devon Scope on the bridge; use it on the Kecleon to reveal and battle it. Now the Fortree Gym is open.

Event 2: DAD checkpoint 3

Six badges in, DAD makes his last road stop: Corviknight, Ludicolo, Gengar, Primeape and Donphan. Exp. Candies L for the win.
