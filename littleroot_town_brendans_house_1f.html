Family dinner: MOM's final battle once UNCLE and UNC G are done. No wild Pokémon in the grass here. Time of day: M = morning (6–10), D = day (10–19), E = evening (19–20), N = night (20–6).

Event: MOM

MOM battles you at home with 2, 5 and 8 badges, then hosts the family dinner battle once UNCLE and UNC G are beaten. Her ace is HUBERT the Lillipup.

MOM: Altaria LV55; Primarina LV55; Toxtricity LV55; Jigglypuff LV57; HUBERT the Lillipup LV58

Team shown for Normal. Hard adds items and full sets; Easy is three levels lower.
