Desert Underpass

‹ Devon Corp: Fossil RevivalQuest: Fossils: Mirage Tower and the Desert Underpass (4 badges, LV25–35) · 6 of 6Back to the side quests ›

TIP

Wild Pokémon here are LV35–45. Time of day: M = morning (6–10), D = day (10–19), E = evening (19–20), N = night (20–6).

Event 1: The other fossil

After you are Champion the Fossil Maniac in Fallarbor opens the tunnel behind his house. Walk to the far end (it is long) for the fossil you did not take from Mirage Tower.

Event 2: Regieleki and the Iron trio

Regieleki stands in the tunnel from eight badges; Iron Treads, Iron Bundle and Iron Hands from eight. Ditto swarm in the wild here, which makes this the best breeding stop in Hoenn.
