TIP

No wild Pokémon in the grass here. Time of day: M = morning (6–10), D = day (10–19), E = evening (19–20), N = night (20–6).

Event 1: Capt. Stern

Stern's shipyard is on the east side, but he is at the Oceanic Museum. Pay the entry fee and head upstairs. The market stalls sell Dolls, a TM and a Powder Jar; the Mart here is where Great Balls appear.
