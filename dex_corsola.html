Corsola

Coral Pokémon
WaterRock
Abilities: Hustle, Natural Cure · hidden: Regenerator
Height: 0.6 m · Weight: 5.0 kg · Gender: 75% female
Egg groups: Water 1 / Water 3 · Catch rate: 60

Corsola live in warm southern seas. If the sea becomes polluted, the beautiful coral stalks become discolored and crumble away in tatters.

Other forms: Galarian Corsola.
