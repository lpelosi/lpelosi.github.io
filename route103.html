TIP

Wild Pokémon here are LV2–4. Time of day: M = morning (6–10), D = day (10–19), E = evening (19–20), N = night (20–6).

Event 1: Rival battle

Your rival waits at the top of the route with the starter that beats yours on type. Level 5, one Pokémon. Use your starter's attacking move and you win; afterwards head back to Littleroot and the lab.

CAUTION

Your rival always picks the starter that counters yours. A second Pokémon from Route 101 makes this easy.
