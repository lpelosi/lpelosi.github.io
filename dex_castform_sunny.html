Castform (Sunny)

Weather Pokémon
Fire
Abilities: Forecast
Height: 0.3 m · Weight: 0.8 kg · Gender: 50% female
Egg groups: Fairy / Amorphous · Catch rate: 45

Castform takes this form on bright days. In an experiment where is was placed in front of a heater, it didn't change to this form. Its body is warm and toasty.

Other forms: Castform (Normal), Castform (Rainy), Castform (Snowy).

‹ PokédexNo. 351

Where to get it

A form of Castform (Normal): get the base Pokémon first, the form change follows.

How to get this form

  • In battle, in certain weather
