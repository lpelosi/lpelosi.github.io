TIP

Wild Pokémon here are LV5–6. Time of day: M = morning (6–10), D = day (10–19), E = evening (19–20), N = night (20–6).

Event 1: UNCLE

Halfway through the woods a Devon researcher is being shaken down by UNCLE, boss of the Frozen Flamingos (this game's Team Magma) and your dad's brother. He fights with a Mime Jr. and a Flamigo, then leaves with a warning about 'finders keepers'. First of five UNCLE fights.

CAUTION

Flamigo is Flying/Fighting and hits hard for its level. Electric, Psychic or Fairy moves handle it; Mime Jr. falls to anything physical.
