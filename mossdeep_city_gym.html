TIP

No wild Pokémon in the grass here. Time of day: M = morning (6–10), D = day (10–19), E = evening (19–20), N = night (20–6).

Event 1: Gym Battle: TATE & LIZA

A double battle against the twins' Psychic teams. Dark and Ghost moves, and anything that hits both targets. Reward: Mind Badge.

CAUTION

Cap is 42. Bring two Pokémon that can take a Psychic hit; Lunatone and Solrock set up Light Screen fast.
