TIP

No wild Pokémon in the grass here. Time of day: M = morning (6–10), D = day (10–19), E = evening (19–20), N = night (20–6).

Event 1: Go home: MOM's first battle

Two badges is the first milestone MOM waits for, and the Knuckle Badge also unlocks Fly in the Field Case. Fly to Littleroot and talk to MOM in the kitchen. She heals your team, then asks for a battle: her Oricorio, Jigglypuff and her ace HUBERT the Lillipup. Win for five Lava Cookies. Optional, but it is the only time her team is this small.

CAUTION

HUBERT never evolves but he is the strongest thing she has and gets Play Rough later. Fighting moves make short work of him.
