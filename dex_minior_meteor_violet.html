Minior (Meteor Violet)

Meteor Pokémon
RockFlying
Abilities: Shields Down
Height: 0.3 m · Weight: 40.0 kg · Gender: Genderless
Egg groups: Mineral · Catch rate: 30

Originally making its home in the ozone layer, it hurtles to the ground when the shell encasing its body grows too heavy. It was born from mutated nanoparticles.

Other forms: Minior (Meteor Red), Minior (Meteor Orange), Minior (Meteor Yellow), Minior (Meteor Green), Minior (Meteor Blue), Minior (Meteor Indigo), Minior (Core Red), Minior (Core Orange), Minior (Core Yellow), Minior (Core Green), Minior (Core Blue), Minior (Core Indigo), Minior (Core Violet).
