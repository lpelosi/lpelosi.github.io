TIP

No wild Pokémon in the grass here. Time of day: M = morning (6–10), D = day (10–19), E = evening (19–20), N = night (20–6).

Event 1: Deliver the Scanner

Give the Scanner to Capt. Stern in his shipyard. He offers a Deep Sea Tooth or a Deep Sea Scale. In this game Clamperl evolves by level anyway, so pick whichever Pokémon you want to hold the item for the stat boost.
