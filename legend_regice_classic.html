Regice

Regice

Level 40 · Ice
Where: Island Cave
Unlock: Sealed Chamber opened (7 badges for Dive)

How to get it

Island Cave on Route 105's western sea. Read the tablet, then walk one full lap around the room hugging the wall without stopping. The door opens; Regice is in the back room.

Emerald's own event, unchanged.

Side quest "The Regi Trio and Regigigas": Island Cave. Reached from: Route 105.
