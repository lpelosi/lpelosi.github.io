Event 1: Family dinner

With eight badges and both siblings beaten, the whole family is in your living room. MOM wants the last word: Altaria, Primarina, Toxtricity, Jigglypuff and HUBERT the Lillipup. Win for an Igglybuff. From then on UNCLE and UNC G live here and offer a daily double battle.
