Pacifidlog Town

‹ Side questsQuest: Pacifidlog, Mirage Island and the trade boat (7 badges, LV35–45) · 1 of 1Back to the side quests ›

TIP

No wild Pokémon in the grass here. Time of day: M = morning (6–10), D = day (10–19), E = evening (19–20), N = night (20–6).

Event 1: Mirage Island

The man in the north-west house can see Mirage Island if one of your party's personality values matches today's number. If he can, sail east to Route 130 for Wynaut and Liechi Berries.

Event 2: Regi hint

The old man in the south-east house tells the Sealed Chamber legend. Useful if you skipped the Braille.

Event 3: Manaphy (Champion)

Manaphy floats by the docks after the Hall of Fame. Level 65 Water; Grass or Electric.
