Audino

Hearing Pokémon
Normal
Abilities: Healer, Regenerator · hidden: Klutz
Height: 1.1 m · Weight: 31.0 kg · Gender: 50% female
Egg groups: Fairy · Catch rate: 255

Its auditory sense is astounding. Using the feelers on its ears, it can tell how someone is feeling or when an egg might hatch.
