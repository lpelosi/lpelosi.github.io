Mr. Mime

Barrier Pokémon
PsychicFairy
Abilities: Soundproof, Filter · hidden: Technician
Height: 1.3 m · Weight: 54.5 kg · Gender: 50% female
Egg groups: Human-Like · Catch rate: 45

A Mr. Mime is a master of pantomime. It can convince others that something unseeable actually exists. Once believed, the imaginary object does become real.

Other forms: Mr (Mime Galar).
