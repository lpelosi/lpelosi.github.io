TIP

Wild Pokémon here are LV19–22. Time of day: M = morning (6–10), D = day (10–19), E = evening (19–20), N = night (20–6).

Event 1: Desert

The sandstorm turns you back until you have the Go-Goggles (from your rival after Lavaridge). The Winstrate family house on the south end is a four-battle gauntlet with a Macho Brace at the end.
