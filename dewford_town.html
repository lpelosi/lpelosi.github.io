TIP

No wild Pokémon in the grass here. Time of day: M = morning (6–10), D = day (10–19), E = evening (19–20), N = night (20–6).

Event 1: Two errands

Steven is deep in Granite Cave with the letter's address on it; the gym is right here. Do the gym first if your team is ready, otherwise the cave gives you a few levels. Dewford Hall trends are cosmetic.
