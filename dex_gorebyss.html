Gorebyss

South Sea Pokémon
Water
Abilities: Swift Swim · hidden: Hydration
Height: 1.8 m · Weight: 22.6 kg · Gender: 50% female
Egg groups: Water 1 · Catch rate: 60

A Gorebyss siphons the body fluids of prey through its thin, tubular mouth. Its light pink body color turns vivid when it finishes feeding.
