Palkia

Spatial Pokémon
WaterDragon
Abilities: Pressure · hidden: Telepathy
Height: 4.2 m · Weight: 336.0 kg · Gender: Genderless
Egg groups: No Eggs Discovered · Catch rate: 3

Palkia has the ability to distort space. Its total control over the boundaries of space enable it to transport itself to faraway places and other dimensions.

Other forms: Palkia (Origin).
