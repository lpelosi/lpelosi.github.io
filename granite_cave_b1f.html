Wild Pokémon here are LV9–11. Time of day: M = morning (6–10), D = day (10–19), E = evening (19–20), N = night (20–6).

Event 1: Cracked floor

The middle of this floor gives way and drops you to B2F. Fall on purpose to reach Steven, or skirt the edges for the items.
