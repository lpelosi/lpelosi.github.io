Event 1: UNCLE's finale

Once the rain and sun have stopped over Sootopolis, UNCLE goes back up Mt. Chimney alone to think. Fly to Lavaridge and take the cable car. He wants one more battle with his real team: Starmie, Weezing, Flamigo, Vanilluxe and his ace Mr. Mime. Beat him and he finally asks what you have that he does not. The answer is sharing; the proof is his Vanillite, which he gives you.

CAUTION

Vanilluxe sets snow and Weezing burns. A Fire or Steel attacker with a Rock move covers the whole team.
