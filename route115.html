TIP

Wild Pokémon here are LV23–26. Time of day: M = morning (6–10), D = day (10–19), E = evening (19–20), N = night (20–6).

Event 1: Meteor Falls' back door

The exit from Meteor Falls comes out here. Rock Smash boulders, a Heal Ball and a stretch of sea to surf north.
